    About Us

    How a simple project became a vision for smarter theater systems

    "What started as a school project became a vision."

    Marquee Cinemas began as a simple POS simulation for academic requirements — but it quickly evolved into something more. We saw not just a grade, but a gap: the need for a minimal, intuitive, and well-structured theater management system that prioritizes usability without unnecessary complexity.

    Today, Marquee Cinemas isn't just a showcase of code — it's a statement of what's possible when curiosity meets clarity. Designed with both functionality and experience in mind, it brings together seamless ticketing, movie browsing, and concession handling in one streamlined platform.

    Built by a student, inspired by real-world systems, and driven by a passion for clean interfaces, Marquee Cinemas stands as a testament to how small ideas can lead to big innovations.
